# graduate-research-career

English | [中文](README.cn.md)

A graduate-school and academic-career guidance skill distilled from research guides, scientist memoirs, mentoring handbooks, and practical training materials.

## Use it for

- Graduate-school onboarding and research habits
- Topic selection, literature reading, and research planning
- Writing, publishing, presenting, and responding to rejection
- Advisor communication, collaboration, and authorship
- Research integrity and data responsibility
- Postdoctoral, faculty, and longer-term career decisions
- AI-assisted research training

## Evidence boundary

Personal stories provide options, not universal laws. Agreement across books is recorded as source convergence, not as experimental proof. Graduation, authorship, misconduct, appeals, and employment requirements must be checked against the applicable institution and year.

## Contents

- `SKILL.md`: stage and problem routing
- `references/01-*.md` to `references/13-*.md`: topic guides
- `references/context-and-policy.md`: experience, consensus, and formal-rule boundary
- `assets/templates/`: meeting, progress, proposal, submission, and defense templates
- `evals/`: behavioral cases

## Core rule

Start from the user's discipline, stage, deadline, support, and constraints. Give one useful next action with a completion signal. Do not replace missing institutional rules with famous quotations or fixed publication counts.
